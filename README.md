# 🛒 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

An end-to-end data analytics project to analyze consumer purchase behavior, uncover spending patterns, identify loyalty segments, and build data-driven business recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Objective

To understand customer shopping behavior and determine:

- Key factors influencing purchase decisions
- Relationship between discounts and spending
- High-value customer segments
- Subscription & loyalty behavior
- Impact of demographics (age, gender) on purchases
- Performance of shipping types & product categories

---

## 📊 Dataset Summary

| Attribute | Details |
|---|---|
Rows | 3,900  
Columns | 18  
Data | Demographics, Purchase details, Ratings, Discounts, Shipping  
Missing Values | 37 in review ratings (handled via median imputation)

---

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Python**: Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib
- **SQL (PostgreSQL)**
- **Power BI**

| Step | Task | Tool |

---

## 🧠 Major Insights

- Loyal customers generate **highest revenue share**
- Discount users still spend **above average**
- **Express shipping** users have higher purchase value
- Subscribers show **higher frequency & AOV**
- Higher product ratings correlate with repeat purchases

---

## 🧾 Key SQL Analyses

- Total revenue by gender
- High-spending discount users
- Top 5 rated products
- Shipping type comparison
- Subscriber vs non-subscriber performance
- Top 3 products per category
- Customer segmentation (New/Returning/Loyal)
